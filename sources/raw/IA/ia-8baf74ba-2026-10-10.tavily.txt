About
By December 1, 2026, certain Iowa Health and Wellness Plan (IHWAP) members must meet monthly requirements to enroll and maintain coverage. These requirements are designed to encourage work and community involvement. Community engagement requirements apply to adults aged 19 to 64 who don't meet an exclusion.
How to Meet New Requirements
There are several ways to meet these new requirements, including working, participating in an education or work program, completing community service, or combining different activities.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
List items for Community Engagement Requirements
When do these rules take effect?
For New Applicants:
These requirements take effect December 1, 2026, for individuals who apply on or after that date.
Applicants must meet the requirement for the month immediately preceding their application month.
For Current Members:
These requirements apply to active IHAWP members with renewal dates on or after February 2027.
Members must meet the requirement for any two months during the review period since their last eligibility determination or renewal.
