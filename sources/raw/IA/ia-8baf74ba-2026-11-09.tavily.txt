IHAWP Community Engagement Requirements
On this page...
About
By December 1, 2026, certain Iowa Health and Wellness Plan (IHWAP) members must meet monthly requirements to enroll and maintain coverage. These requirements are designed to encourage work and community involvement.
Community engagement requirements apply to adults aged 19 to 64 who don't meet an exclusion.

Exclusions
Some people on the IHAWP program are excluded from these new requirements.
List items for IHAWP CER Exclusion List
Pregnancy or Postpartum
Who qualifies
You are excluded from the community engagement requirement if you are currently pregnant or received postpartum coverage through Iowa Medicaid within the last 12 months.
Examples of proof
You can provide a written statement or a completed Community Engagement Declaration form. You can also report a current pregnancy by calling your eligibility worker.
What must you provide?
If you report a current pregnancy, provide your due date and the number of babies you are expecting.
Parent, guardian, caretaker relative or family caregiver for a child under age 14 or a person with a disability
Who qualifies
You may be excluded if you provide regular, ongoing care to a child under age 14 or a person with a disability.
Examples of proof
You can provide:
What must the proof show?
Your proof must show that the care is regular and not incidental.
If you are not related to the child or person with a disability and do not live with them, your proof must also show the number of hours of care you provide each month.
Inmate of a public institution
Who qualifies
You are excluded if:
Examples of proof
You can provide a letter from your probation or parole officer, a booking record, or a court record.
What must the proof show?
Your proof must show the dates of your incarceration or participation in a work release program.
Drug or alcohol treatment program
Who qualifies
You are excluded if you participate in an inpatient or outpatient drug or alcohol addiction treatment program that meets the program requirements.
The program must be operated by a qualifying private nonprofit organization or institution, or a publicly operated community mental health center, and provide treatment intended to help people recover from drug or alcohol addiction.
Examples of proof
You can provide a letter from the program or your counselor, confirmation of enrollment or attendance, or a discharge summary.
What must the proof show?
Your proof must confirm that the program meets the requirements and show your dates of participation.
Disabled, medically frail, or serious medical condition
Who qualifies
You are excluded if you are blind or disabled, have a substance use disorder or disabling mental disorder, have a physical, intellectual, or developmental disability, or have a serious or complex medical condition that prevents you from completing 80 hours per month of approved activities.
Your medical provider must confirm that your condition prevents you from meeting the 80-hour monthly requirement for work, education, volunteering, or other approved activities.
Examples of proof
You can provide a written statement or a completed Community Engagement Declaration form.
You can also report a disability, medical frailty, or serious medical condition by calling your eligibility worker.

When do these rules take effect?
For New Applicants:
These requirements take effect December 1, 2026, for individuals who apply on or after that date.
Applicants must meet the requirement for the month immediately preceding their application month.
For Current Members:
These requirements apply to active IHAWP members with renewal dates on or after February 2027.
Members must meet the requirement for any two months during the review period since their last eligibility determination or renewal.
