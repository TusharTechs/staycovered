IHAWP Community Engagement Requirements
About IHAWP Community Engagement Requirements
In July 2025, the federal Working Families Tax Cut (WFTC) law was enacted, introducing federal work requirements called ‘community engagement.’ Iowa will adjust our work requirements implementation to align with this federal law, which differs slightly from the state legislation.
If you want to see if this applies to you, visit our Self-Service Portal and chat with Ellie, our virtual assistant.

How do I know if I qualify for IHAWP?
