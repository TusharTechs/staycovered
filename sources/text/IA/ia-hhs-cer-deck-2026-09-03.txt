What are Community Engagement Requirements?
Starting December 1, 2026, certain members enrolled in the Iowa Health and Wellness Plan (IHAWP) must meet monthly Community Engagement Requirements to enroll in and maintain their health coverage. These requirements are intended to encourage employment, education, job training, volunteer service, and other forms of community involvement. While some states refer to these as "work requirements," Iowa uses the term Community Engagement Requirements.
Exclusions Some people on the IHAWP program do not have to complete community engagement activities:  You are pregnant or had a baby in the last 12 months while on Iowa Medicaid  You care for a child under 14 or someone with a disability  You are or were recently in jail or prison  You are in a drug or alcohol treatment program  You have a disability, are medically frail, or have a serious medical condition  You already meet work requirements for SNAP or FIP  You are a veteran with a total disability rating  You are an American Indian or Alaska Native
