Appendix B – OPTIONAL at Application Submission
Community Engagement Exclusions Form This form is optional at application submission. DPHHS will attempt to verify exclusions using existing data sources. If verification is unsuccessful, you will receive an additional request to provide verifications for qualifying activities through Appendix B.
Individuals who meet an approved exclusion are not required to perform and report income or activity hours to maintain their Medicaid Expansion coverage. This form is required for each individual in the Medicaid Expansion household between the ages of 19 and 64 who is reporting a possible exclusion. Verification of exclusions may be required. Meeting Community Engagement Requirements through an Exclusion: There are two different ways you may be excluded from the community engagement requirement, and they work differently: • •
If you are a specified excluded individual, the requirement does not apply to you at all. You must show that you are a specified excluded individual at the time of application. If you have an exception or a short-term hardship, you are treated as meeting the requirement only for the months that the exception or hardship applies to you. At application, you must show that these apply to you in the month before your application. For more information, please visit medicaidchanges.mt.gov.
Verifications: •
The Department will first attempt to verify any exclusions through existing data sources. If verification is unsuccessful, you will receive an additional request to provide verifications for qualifying activities through Appendix B. Additional details on what type(s) of verifications are allowed for each exclusion are summarized on the Community Engagement Exclusions Summary Form (page 2). Please visit medicaidchanges.mt.gov for a full list of exclusions and approved verifications.
