Appendix A – OPTIONAL at Application Submission
Medicaid Community Engagement Activity Requirements Reporting Form This form is optional at application submission. DPHHS will attempt to verify participation in qualifying activities using existing data sources. If verification is unsuccessful, you will receive an additional request to provide verifications for qualifying activities through Appendix A.
All individuals between the ages of 19 and 64 must meet the community engagement requirement as a condition of eligibility for Medicaid Expansion. Individuals must demonstrate that they are participating in at least 80 hours per month in one or more of the following activities, or demonstrate that they have an exclusion (see Appendix B: Community Engagement Exclusions Form): • • • • • •
Working: A job where you receive income, other compensation, or are unpaid (paid work may be verified through existing data sources) Community service or volunteering: Community service with a not-for-profit organization with a valid Tax ID Workforce training or job readiness programs: Participating in an approved state or federal work program Internships or registered apprenticeships: Unpaid work, participating in a program approved by the Department Going to school: Enrolled in an institution of higher education, career and technical education program, or high school/GED program Mixing activities: You can combine activities. For example, you could work 60 hours and complete 20 hours of community service
You must show that you have met the community engagement requirement: At application: The month before your new application At redetermination: At least 3 months since your last redetermination (the months do not need to be consecutive) Some individuals are excluded from community engagement requirements: Individuals who meet an approved exclusion are not required to meet the community engagement requirements for one or more months. If you think you might qualify for an exclusion, see Appendix B. What we need from you: To aid in the application process, begin gathering proof that you meet any of the above requirements. You can use documents like pay stubs, community service hours verified by the organization, or a school schedule that shows credit hours. Providing documentation at application submission is optional; however, you may receive an additional request for verifications after your application is processed and checked against existing data sources. For more information on what activities qualify, please visit medicaidchanges.mt.gov. • •
Getting Help: The Department of Labor & Industry and DPHHS’ Vocational Rehab programs can help you find training, internships, and programs to get you ready for work. These programs may count toward your 80-hour goal. For more information, please visit medicaidchanges.mt.gov.
