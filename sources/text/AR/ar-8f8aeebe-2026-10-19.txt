ARHOME Community Engagement and Work Requirement
Spanish | Marshallese
Finding a job brings purpose, meaning, and economic independence, which can lead to better health. That’s why DHS will launch a Community Engagement and Work Requirement for certain beneficiaries who receive Medicaid through ARHOME.
This means you will need to work, volunteer, or take classes for a combined total of 80 hours per month to keep your benefits beginning on January 1, 2027. DHS launched a soft implementation of the requirement on July 1, 2026, but no penalties will be in effect until January 1, 2027.
If you or someone you know is between the ages of 19 and 64 and is served by ARHOME, the Community Engagement and Work Requirement may affect your benefits. More details about the changes will be shared soon, but the best thing to do now is make sure you can receive important updates and other information.

What To Do Now
It’s easy – right now you should update your contact information, and sign up for text and email alerts through Access Arkansas to ensure you receive the latest information.
Here’s how to update your contact information:
1. Go online at access.arkansas.gov or ar.gov/accessanywhere
2. Call the Access Arkansas hotline at 1-855-372-1084
3. Click Here to watch a short video on how to sign up for text and email alerts
