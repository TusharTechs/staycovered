The Arkansas Department of Human Services notified all ARHOME recipients of their status at the start of the soft launch of the Community Engagement and Work Requirement based on automated checks of data available at that time. We expect that many of these statuses will change when the full program begins next year as new data sources are added, processes are finalized, and the Customer Service Center comes online to proactively reach out to beneficiaries and request necessary documentation. The messages below are samples of emails that were sent to beneficiaries based on their status at the start of the soft launch:
Currently not meeting requirement You recently received a notice that said you are enrolled in the Arkansas Health and Opportunity for Me (ARHOME) program and are currently not meeting the Community Engagement and Work Requirement that asks you to work, volunteer, or take classes for a total of 80 hours per month. We are in a “soft launch” right now, which means there no penalties for not meeting the new requirements. You will not lose or be denied coverage now due to not meeting the new requirement. Think of the soft launch as practice – it’s a chance to learn how to meet the requirements now so you will be ready when they start on January 1, 2027. Starting then, you may lose or be denied your benefits if you do not meet the requirements and you are not exempt. We understand this may be a big change. We want to help beneficiaries find jobs and build careers that bring purpose, meaning, and financial independence, which can lead to better health. We’ve built a new webpage at ar.gov/engage that will be updated regularly with all the information you need to know and Frequently Asked Questions, or FAQs. And you can visit the Arkansas Workforce Connections website for programs that provide support and guidance to help you build and grow your career. You can also learn on our website if you are exempt from the new requirements. Common exemptions include being younger than 19, older than 64, pregnant, caring for young children, and others. The full list of exemptions is on the website. Along with the webpage, there are other resources available to help you. You can visit access.arkansas.gov to update your contact information and upload important documents. You can also call your local DHS county office or the Customer Service Center at 1-855-3721084, or visit ar.gov/accessanywhere to submit a question online.
No matter how you need to reach us, we’re here to support you.
