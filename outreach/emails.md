## Version A: navigator / assister organizations (HCAN)

**Subject:** 10-minute feedback request: free tool for Medicaid work-requirement paperwork (Nebraska)

Hello,

I'm a software developer building a free, open-source tool to help Medicaid navigators and assisters with the new work requirements, starting with Nebraska.

What it does today, on synthetic test cases only:

- It reads a client's notice and their pay stubs or gig-app earnings statements.
- It builds a month-by-month worksheet against the 80-hour / $580 test. The arithmetic is done by plain code, not AI.
- Every number links back to the exact line in the client's document.
- It flags issues such as gross gig payouts before expenses, or weeks that cross two months.
- Every rule it cites is quoted from DHHS or CMS text, as of a specific date. When the official pages change, it flags which open cases need a second look.

It does not decide eligibility, does not submit anything to the state, and has never been used with real client data.

Would someone on your team be willing to spend 10–15 minutes looking at it, or answering three questions?

1. Where do your assisters lose the most time on work-requirement cases right now?
2. Is turning gig or seasonal income into monthly hours or income actually hard in practice?
3. What would make you trust, or distrust, a tool like this?

Any answer helps, even "this wouldn't be useful". I won't quote anyone publicly without explicit permission.

Thank you for the work you're doing,
